User Manual

Everything you need to know to get the most out of Mivio.

1. Connect your Server (SMB/WebDAV)

Go to the "Servers" tab and tap the "+" button. Choose your protocol (SMB for Windows/Mac/NAS, WebDAV for remote servers). Enter your IP address, port, username, and password. Mivio will automatically scan the folders you select.

2. Automatic Metadata

When you add a server, Mivio will try to match your video files with TMDB. For best results, name your files clearly (e.g., "Movie Name (2023).mp4" or "Show S01E01.mkv"). If a match is wrong, you can long-press the item and select "Fix Match".

3. Subtitles

During playback, tap the subtitle icon. You can select an embedded subtitle track, a local file, or tap "Search Online" to download subtitles from OpenSubtitles automatically based on the video hash.

4. Player Controls

Swipe left/right to seek. Swipe up/down on the left side to adjust brightness, and on the right side for volume. Double tap the edges to skip 10 seconds.
