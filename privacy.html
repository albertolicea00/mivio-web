Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Mivio is designed with your privacy in mind. We believe that your media library is your business, not ours. Therefore, we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal media files, server IP addresses, or watch history to our servers.

All media parsing, metadata fetching, and playback tracking happen entirely locally on your device or via your personal, self-hosted server (e.g., Plex, Jellyfin, Emby).

2. Telemetry and Crash Reports

To improve the stability and performance of the Mivio applications across all platforms, we may collect anonymous crash logs and basic telemetry (such as device model and OS version). This data contains no personally identifiable information and is solely used for debugging.

You can opt-out of crash reporting at any time within the application settings.

3. Third-Party Services (APIs)

Mivio interfaces with third-party APIs to enhance your media experience:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): Used to automatically download movie posters, synopses, and cast information.
  • OpenSubtitles: Used to search and download subtitles based on video file hashes.

When the app contacts these services, your device's IP address and search queries are subject to their respective Privacy Policies.

4. Cookies and Web Tracking

The Mivio website (this landing page and help center) uses strictly necessary local storage to remember your preferences (like the Dark/Light theme toggle). We do not use tracking cookies, tracking pixels, or intrusive third-party analytics.

You can browse our website without worrying about being tracked across the internet.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official community channels or GitHub repository.
